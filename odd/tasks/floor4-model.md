# Fourth-floor measured 3D model

## Goal and scope
Reconstruct the fourth-floor office in `plano_oficina.pdf` as measured, metre-based Blender geometry, with matching GLB checkpoints for eventual Three.js integration. Work zone by zone and preserve a resumable `.blend` at every accepted checkpoint. Keep the prior `office.glb`/`src/office-plan.json` untouched until the replacement validates; they are an approximate demo, not a measurement authority.

In scope: floor outline, true cut walls, openings, circulation, stairs/elevators, rooms, then desks and chairs as static geometry. Keep furniture/equipment separate from walls and delay it until wall sections are validated. Fixed desks are 0.75 m high. Maintain 2.40 m minimum clear ceiling height; use 2.40 m for full-height walls while preserving explicit partial-height wall annotations. Omit/disable the ceiling slab in the web GLB so the plan remains visible from above. No seat booking, backend, occupancy logic, or photorealism in this phase.

- User supplied minimum ceiling clearance 2.40 m and fixed desk height 0.75 m.
- Use PDF linework/labels for remaining geometry. Separate directly dimensioned values from scale-traced values and any assumptions; do not silently present assumptions as surveyed data.
- Preserve the old GLB until the replacement passes validation. Do not mutate or overwrite unrelated Blender objects/assets.
